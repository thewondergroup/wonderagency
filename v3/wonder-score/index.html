Illustrative example

When a guest asks an AI where to go, there are no ten blue links. There’s one answer.

Search used to give people a page of results to choose from. AI assistants give a short answer with a few names in it, and most people go with it. If you’re not named, that guest never sees you.

Wonder Score measures how visible you are in those answers, analyses your website and your competitors, and gives you a clear list of what to change to get recommended more. Then, if you want, we do the work for you.
