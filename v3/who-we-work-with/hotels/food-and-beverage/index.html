Hotels

Hotel food and beverage marketing

Turn the hotel restaurant and bar into somewhere locals choose, not just somewhere guests eat because they’re staying.

Get a free audit →What’s included

Most hotel restaurants are marketed to people who are already in the building.

Guests will usually eat in at least once. The opportunity is everyone else: locals, office workers, people celebrating, people who’d never think of a hotel restaurant as a night out. Win them and the restaurant and bar become a reason to visit, and often a reason to stay.

We market hotel restaurants and bars as destinations in their own right, with their own content, accounts, offers and search presence, while keeping the link to the hotel clear. It’s the approach behind one hotel group’s 58% year-on-year rise in F&B revenue in three months.

One shoot day. A month of everything.
