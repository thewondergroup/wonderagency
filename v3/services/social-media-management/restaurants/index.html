Social media management

Social media management for restaurants

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook run day to day for your restaurant: posts and reels that make people hungry, replies to every comment and message, and reporting tied to covers.

Get a free audit →What’s included

Diners check your Instagram before they book. It has to look like the best meal they’ll have this week.

Your feed is your menu, your dining room and your reviews in one place. People look at the food, the room and how busy you seem, and decide in seconds whether you’re worth the trip.

We run restaurant accounts around the rhythm of service: new dishes as they land, the specials that need selling, quiet nights that need filling, and the moments regulars love. Every post has a job, and every enquiry in the comments or messages reaches your team.

Your accounts, run like they matter.
