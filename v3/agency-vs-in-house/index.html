Agency vs in-house

Agency or in-house marketing?

An honest comparison for restaurants, hotels and bars. Both can work. The right answer depends on what you need covered, and many venues end up doing both.

The real choice isn’t a salary against a fee. It’s how much of the job gets done well.

Hospitality marketing now covers photography, video, social media, paid ads, search, AI search, websites and email. That’s a lot to ask of one person, however good they are, and the gaps usually end up filled by freelancers, tools and the general manager’s evenings.

An agency gives you a team that covers every channel and has seen what works across many venues. An in-house marketer gives you someone on site every day. If you can, the strongest setup is often both: someone in the building, and a specialist team behind them.
