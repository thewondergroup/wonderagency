About us

A digital agency that specialises in hospitality

The Wonder Agency is a London marketing agency for restaurants, hotels, bars and food and drink brands. One team runs content, social media, ads, search, websites and email, with one goal: more bookings.

Get a free audit →Our services

We work with Michelin-starred restaurants, hotel groups and independents across the UK and internationally.

Founded by Charles Morris and Charlotte Giles, The Wonder Agency is part of The Wonder Group. We specialise in hospitality because it’s what we know best: what makes people choose one place over another, and what turns a follower into a booking.

Hospitality is our specialism, not a limit. The same approach works for other consumer brands who want the same standard, and we take that work on selectively.

What we believe

01

People choose places that say something about them.

Where we eat, drink and stay says something about who we are. Good hospitality marketing makes clear what choosing your venue says about your guests, and makes sure your whole team tells the same story.

02

Attention is an exchange.

People give you their attention and expect something back: something useful, something beautiful or something to look forward to. We make content that earns its place in the feed.

03

True stories beat spin.

Storytelling is the oldest way people share what matters. We tell the real story of your food, your rooms and your people, because guests can tell the difference.
